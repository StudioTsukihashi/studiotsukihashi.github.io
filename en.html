Our Philosophy

Small, close-knit and deeply connected to our stories

Studio Tsukihashi is an independent authors’ studio built around the original works of a novelist and a manga writer.

We deliberately want to remain small and personal. The studio may grow, but only with people who share our values and enrich our stories as artists, illustrators, character designers, letterers or translators.

“We do not grow simply to become bigger. We grow so that we can tell better stories.”
